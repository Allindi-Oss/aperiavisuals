№ 01 · Discipline

Food & Beverage.

Editorial photography for Nairobi's hospitality scene — restaurants, breweries, and chefs who treat the plate as a frame.

Beer-battered cod Bonds · 35mm · f/2.8 · 1/200s
Food photography isn't still life. It's a fifteen-minute window — between the chef plating and the diner cutting in — where steam, gloss, and freshness are at their peak.

We shoot in restaurants, on real service, with real plates. No food stylists faking textures with motor oil and cardboard. The light is what's there. The garnish is what the kitchen sent out.

What you see in the frame is what your guest will see when they sit down — only better composed, better lit, and held still long enough to look at.

Day shoots are typical. Half-day enough for a focused tasting menu. Available for menu refreshes, OTA hero shots, brand campaigns, and editorial features.
