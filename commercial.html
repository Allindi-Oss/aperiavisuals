N° 001

Tamuwa Limited.

Sector · Biomass · briquettes
Frames · 06

Two sites in one brief — out where the raw material is collected, and back inside the plant where it becomes fuel.

The exterior frames are loose and observational; the interior ones are quieter, closer, more deliberate. The colour breaks — high-vis yellow, neon green tape, blue machinery — were already there.

Asili Coffee Farm.

Sector · Smallholder coffee

Scale from above, person below — the shortest possible read on a coffee brand.

Oltotoi Ranch.

Sector · Livestock · feedlot
Frames · 09

A full day, told in the order it happened — aerial first, then trucks, hands, troughs, faces.

A picture essay rather than a campaign. The arc moves from infrastructure into supply, and finally into the long covered feed alley where most of the day happens.
